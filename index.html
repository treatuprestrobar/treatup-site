A Pursuit of Excellence

❧

Founded in 2019, Treat Up Restaurant & Bar brings a fresh focus on flavor to Biratnagar. Born from a simple yet powerful mission: to bring uncompromising quality to the table in Airport Mode. We wanted to create more than just a place to eat; we envisioned a sanctuary where culinary art meets warm hospitality.

7+ Years of
Excellence

From a local dream into a beloved community staple, our growth has been fueled by the smiles of our guests and the dedication of our team. Every ingredient is selected with care, and every recipe tells a story of tradition reimagined for the modern palate.

Chef's Philosophy

"Quality is not just a standard; it is our core priority. Every dish must be a masterpiece of taste, crafted with patience and precision. We believe that great food has the power to bring people together and create lasting memories."

Rajan Dahal Head Chef & Owner
